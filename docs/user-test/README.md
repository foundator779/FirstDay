# FirstDay Go: a 15-minute user test

A short, honest test with 3–5 real people before the deadline. It answers two questions judges asked: does someone who
isn't us get from a real Bee recording to practice without help, and does the Change Drill make sense to them?

**Who to ask** (aim for at least one of each): an adult with ADHD who has started a job in the last few years; someone
who trains new staff (café, retail, warehouse, front desk); a job coach or ADHD coach.

**What you need:** their own phone or laptop, the live app (`https://d1ypavau8ae868.cloudfront.net`), a timer, and
this page. Do it in person or on a video call with their screen shared. **Don't help** unless they're stuck for a full
minute; if you do, write down where.

## 1. Before you start (read this to them)

> I'm testing an app, not you. Think out loud: say what you're looking for and what surprises you. There are no
> wrong answers. I'll write down what you do and one quote, without your full name. Is that OK?

Write down: first name or initial, role (new hire / trainer / coach), ADHD yes/no/prefer not to say (only if they
offer it). Nothing else.

## 2. Tasks (start the timer at Task A)

| Task | Say this | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| A. Sign up | "Open this link and make an account." | They're on Today |
| B. Real Bee data | "There's a real conversation recorded on an Apple Watch. Turn it into practice." | The 3 steps are confirmed (time this: **time to first step**) |
| C. Practise | "Practise for your shift." | They finish one practice session |
| D. A rule changes | "Your trainer just changed a rule. Find out what changed and practise it." (They replay "Rosa's quick update" and add it to the training.) | They finish the Change Drill |
| E. My record | "Your trainer praised you today. Keep it for your next review." (They paste the praise line from the README's judge step 6, keep the win, and open My record.) | They tap **Copy for my review** |

## 3. What to write down (one row per person)

| Field | How |
|---|---|
| Time to first step | Minutes from Task A's start to the 3 steps being confirmed |
| Finished practice | Yes / No |
| Finished the Change Drill | Yes / No / Needed help |
| Picked the old answer in the drill | Yes / No (the trap is the gray bin) |
| Stuck points | Where they paused over 20 s or asked for help, in their words |
| Ease (1–5) | "How easy was that, 1 to 5?" |
| Would use it | "Would you use this in your first week at a job? Why or why not?" |
| One quote | The most honest thing they said, word for word |

## 4. After

- Put each row in `docs/user-test/results.csv` (same fields). Keep the stuck points even when they're unflattering.
- Report all of them in the README's "What testers did" section, with the count of people, the median time to first
  step, how many finished the drill, and every stuck point. Five people with honest friction notes is worth more than
  one glowing quote.
- Don't report outcomes this test can't show (retention, fewer errors at work). Say what was measured and nothing more.
